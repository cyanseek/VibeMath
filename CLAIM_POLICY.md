# Claim policy

VibeMath reports evidence states. It does not independently declare new mathematical results.

## Preferred words

The interface may use these when the corresponding structured record exists:

- tracked
- reported
- candidate
- source-audited
- site-reproduced
- mechanically verified
- Lean-checked
- independently expert-verified
- partial
- contested
- retracted
- replay-ready

## Restricted words

Do not use these as unqualified VibeMath judgments:

- solved
- proved
- disproved
- first
- independent
- novel
- peer reviewed

They may appear inside an attributed source report or when structured evidence supports the exact claim. The page must display the supporting source and verification boundary nearby.

## Non-negotiable rules

- `candidate` never auto-upgrades to `resolved`.
- A source's status is not silently replaced by an aggregate.
- Lean checking does not automatically establish statement fidelity.
- Peer review is separate from mathematical correctness.
- Replay-ready is an actionability rule, not a success forecast.
- New agent or Low-Hanging Fruit output enters as candidate evidence.
- VibeMath never claims complete coverage of mathematics.
